Who we are

glide. is made by Yehia Waleed Development ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on Google Play (Android phones and tablets), Google Play Games on PC, and Steam (Windows). If you have a question about it, the contact section says how to reach us.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress locally: high scores, distance records, coins, ranks, unlocked items, missions, challenges, achievements, your settings and any levels you build. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We do not run servers for glide. and have no copy of your save.

Level sharing works with codes you copy and paste yourself. The game reads your clipboard only when you tap Import, and only to load the level code you copied.

To delete your saved data, use Records → Reset the whole game in the menu, or uninstall the game.

Ads (Android and Google Play Games on PC)

The free Android version shows optional rewarded ads. You only see one if you choose to, for example by tapping Revive after a crash. To have an ad ready, the game starts Google's ad service when it opens and loads an ad in the background.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • device information such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostics about the ad service

If you agree, Google may use this to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, or if you're in a region where consent is required and you decline, you still get ads, but they're not personalised. Ads in glide. are limited to content suitable for teens.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising ID or any of this data ourselves. We only see totals in AdMob, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). On first launch, a Google consent form asks how ads may use your data. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads). Google's My Ad Center controls ad personalisation across Google.
  • No ads at all. The Steam version has no ads. On Android, ads only play when you choose to watch one.

In-app purchases

glide. sells optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

Payments are made through your Google Play account, and Google handles them under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never see your card, bank or billing details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play (which item and an order number), so it can check the purchase is genuine and add what you bought to your save. Refunds go through Google Play.

If purchases are added to the Steam version, Steam handles payment in the same way, and we likewise only receive confirmation of the purchase.

Steam (Windows)

The Steam version shows no ads and sends no data to us. Steam itself is run by Valve, and your Steam account is covered by Valve's Privacy Policy.

Children

glide. is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Android version and you have a concern, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit how it's used, and to withdraw consent.

Because we don't hold personal data about you, most requests are handled by the controls above or by Google, which processes the ad data. For ads, the legal basis is your consent where the law requires it. We don't sell your personal information. If you need help with a request, contact us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Security and retention

Your save stays on your device until you reset the game or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and protected under Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
