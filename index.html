glide.

A calm, fast endless runner. Steer, boost, thread the gaps. Just one more run.

Get it on Google Play Steam Coming soon

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Guide your cube down an endless track, slip through the gaps between blocks and chain near misses to push your multiplier higher. Every run is quick to start and hard to stop.

Steer, boost, brake

Hold boost for double points. Brake when it gets tight. The closer you cut it, the more you score.

Always something to chase

New missions every run, challenges that change how you play, ranks, a daily bonus and global leaderboards.

Make it yours

Collect cubes, hats, trails and worlds as you get better.

Plays anywhere

Phones, tablets, foldables, Chromebooks and Google Play Games on PC. Works offline.

Free to play No pop-up ads, no banners Only ads you choose to watch
